How we can help

Our Services

Property care and outdoor projects across the Cariboo region.

🌿

Yard & Property Cleanup

Get your outdoor space back under control with seasonal cleanup, brush and debris piles, and general property tidying. Share photos and the size of the area for a more useful estimate.

🪵

Fencing & Repairs

Fence installation projects, damaged sections, posts, gates, and repairs. We’ll discuss materials, measurements, access, and the finish you’re looking for.

🛠️

General Maintenance

Practical hands-on property maintenance. Describe the task and we’ll confirm whether it fits our services and what the next steps look like.

🌲

Tree & Branch Removal

Help with branches and vegetation cleanup. Tree work can carry serious risks, so the size, condition, and location of the tree must be assessed before confirming the scope.

🚜

Land & Outdoor Projects

Outdoor improvements and land projects based on your goals, access, terrain, and site conditions. Send a description and photos to start the conversation.

❄️

Snow Removal

Seasonal snow clearing for driveways and walkways. Include your location, driveway length, and whether you’re looking for one-time or recurring service.

Simple process

From idea to done.

  • 1. Tell us about it. Share the job, location, timing, and photos if possible.
  • 2. Confirm the details. We’ll discuss access, scope, materials, and pricing.
  • 3. Get the work underway. Once the details are agreed, we’ll arrange the next steps.
